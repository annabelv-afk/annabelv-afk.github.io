Everything you need to know about SOLV wellness strips — from what's inside to how to use them at 35,000 feet.

The product

A SOLV strip is a thin, dissolvable oral film that you place on your tongue. It melts in seconds — no water, no pills, no mess. Each formula is precisely dosed and designed specifically for the conditions your body faces during air travel.

Tear open the pouch, place the strip on your tongue, and let it dissolve — it takes about 10–15 seconds. You don't need water or any other liquid. The strip can be used any time before, during, or after your flight.

Energy (Strawberry Mint) — combines Green Coffee Bean Extract, L-Theanine, and Vitamin B12 for a smooth, focused lift without the crash. Ideal for early-morning departures and same-day meetings.

Sleep (Cranberry) — a blend of Valerian, Melatonin, Lavender, Chamomile, and Hibiscus formulated to ease your body into sleep on long-haul flights.

Hydration (Lemon Peppermint) — Vitamin D, Collagen, Vitamin C, and Zinc support skin, immune function, and cellular hydration to counter the dry cabin air.

The Energy and Hydration strips pair well together and are designed to be used on the same journey. We recommend avoiding the Sleep strip simultaneously with Energy as they work in opposite directions. If you have any health conditions or take medications, consult your healthcare provider before use.

Ingredients & safety

The strips use a plant-based film base (pullulan) to deliver active ingredients. They are free from gluten, artificial colors, and high-fructose corn syrup. Full ingredient lists are available on each product page.

Yes — all three SOLV formulas are vegan and gluten-free. The Hydration strip contains Collagen; if you are vegan and prefer to avoid it, the Energy and Sleep strips do not contain any animal-derived ingredients.

Travel & TSA

Yes. SOLV strips are dry, solid items — they are not subject to TSA liquid restrictions. You can keep them in any pocket, bag, or carry-on without needing to place them in your liquids bag.
