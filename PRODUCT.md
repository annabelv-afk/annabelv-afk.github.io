# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Frequent and occasional air travelers, 25–45, health-conscious, often time-poor. They feel the physical cost of flying — jet lag, fatigue, poor circulation, disrupted sleep — and accept it as inevitable. Business travelers on back-to-back trips, leisure travelers who want to arrive well. Context: browsing on desktop or mobile, often post-booking or pre-departure. They are skeptical of supplement hype but open to something credible and effortless.

## Product Purpose

SOLV makes dissolvable wellness strips designed specifically for air travel — three formulas: Sleep (Rest), Circulation (Flow), and Energy (Boost). Pre-launch DTC site; buttons capture email for launch notification. The site's job: convince a skeptical traveler that SOLV is different, credible, and worth waiting for. Success = email captured or intent to purchase the moment orders open.

## Brand Personality

Warm, approachable, trusted. Like a smart founder talking to you directly — not selling at you, just solving a problem you already have. Science-backed confidence without clinical coldness. The product should feel like a premium treat, not a chore or a prescription.

## Anti-references

- Generic pharma / pill-bottle aesthetic: clinical white, blue-cross health vibes, CVS pharmacy energy. SOLV is not a pharmacy shelf product.

## Design Principles

1. **Confidence without noise.** The science and the product do the talking. No hyperbole, no exclamation marks, no breathless claims.
2. **Premium feels effortless.** The ritual is simple — one strip, no water. The design should feel just as frictionless. No complexity, no visual clutter.
3. **Warmth through specificity.** Not "wellness for travelers" vagueness — specific problems, specific ingredients, specific moments in a journey. Warm because it's real.
4. **Travel-native palette.** The dark, rich colors (deep maroon, night tones) echo cabin lighting and nighttime flights — not a sterile lab or a spa.
5. **Show the product.** The tins and strips are distinctive and beautiful. Let them lead.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. No specific stated requirements. Color accents are high-contrast against dark backgrounds. Reduced-motion preference should be respected for stat animations and modal transitions.
